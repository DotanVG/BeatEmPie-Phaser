Desktop controls

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Aim with the mouse and click to drop a pie at the cursor, or press Space to auto-target. Select pies with 1–0, cycle with Q / E, dash with Shift, and pause with P / Esc.

Touch controls

Move with the on-screen joystick. Tap the arena to drop a pie, or use the pie button to auto-target. Tap a pie slot to select it, use the curved arrows to cycle pies, the dash button to dash, and the pause button to pause.

Play in a web browser with a keyboard and mouse or a touch screen. On phones and tablets, rotate to landscape to play.
